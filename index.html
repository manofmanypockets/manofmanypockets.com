About Josh:

Hailing from Melbourne's south-east, Josh is the driving low-end force of Melbourne's heavy outfit, Bitter Kind. Josh has been playing music since he was little, and picked up the bass guitar at age 10, and now, 20-something years later, he has cemented himself as a multi-genre specialist with the ambition to continuously improve upon his foundations. Josh has over 5 years in the professional field, he has undertaken session work for EDM producers, fill-in work for jazz-fusion and blues outfits, and everything in between. If you need a go-to-guy for anything bass related, he's your guy!
